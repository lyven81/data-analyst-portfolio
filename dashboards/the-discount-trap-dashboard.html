What to add next
New products the numbers favour

  • High-margin add-ons (fries, dips, drinks, dessert)

    Order size and profit per order are the real scoreboard; add-ons lift both at low ingredient cost, without the shrink a discount causes.

  • Combo bundles (burger + side + drink; 2-pizza family deal)

    Order size is capped by the menu; bundles raise it without a straight price cut.

  • A mid-price specialty pizza (~RM 28–32, under 30% cost)

    Pizza earns about twice per order and survives the 30% app cut; another Pepperoni-profile pizza compounds the strongest line.

  • A chicken-based premium burger to replace the Double Cheeseburger

    Chicken burgers run the lowest ingredient cost (28–30%) against the cheeseburger's 36%; same price, more profit.

  • A weekend "feast" bundle

    Orders are bigger on weekends; a weekend-only premium bundle captures that higher spend.
