What to do
Inventory, staff and festive planning

Prep to the peak

Stock and prep the weekend and the 11am rush hardest; run lean on weekday afternoons. Kitchen ready before 11.

Staff to the week

Full crew Saturday and Sunday and across late morning; a light crew Monday and Friday and in the afternoon lull.

Scale down for festivals

Cut prep and hours for Chinese New Year, Christmas and Deepavali weeks; expect 20-33% fewer orders, not more.
